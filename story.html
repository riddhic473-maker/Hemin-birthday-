Chapter One

Two years of us

♡

Hemin,

I still remember the day we met. I remember each and every little detail — how our conversation started, what we talked about, and everything that happened that day.

And I still light up whenever someone asks me how we met. I will very, very happily tell them the entire story, from the very beginning, with all the little details. I don't think I will ever get bored of telling that story.

In fact, I will shamelessly take the credit for our relationship every single time because, obviously, I deserve some credit for getting us here.

Two years later, I am very, very, veryyy happy that I have you. I honestly cannot imagine a single day without you.

I don't think I have spent a single day since our first meeting without thinking about you in some way. Somehow, without even realizing when it happened, you became such a huge part of my everyday life.

I love you so, so, so much.

I love what we have, I love the person you are, and I love the life I imagine when I think about us in the future.

More than anything, I want us to have the most loving family. One that is full of love, comfort, laughter, ridiculousness, cuddles, and all the little ordinary things that we currently have to miss because of the distance.

I would do anything for us to have that.

And until we get there, I'll keep choosing you, loving you, missing you, annoying you, and very proudly telling everyone the story of how we met.

Because that story will always be one of my favourites.

And I hope we get to keep adding chapters to it for a very, very long time. ♡
