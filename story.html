Chapter One

Two years of us

♡

Hemin,

Two years is such a strange thing to think about. Somehow it feels like yesterday, and at the same time, I can't remember what my life looked like before you.

We've had conversations that lasted for hours, moments that lasted seconds, and so many memories made from miles apart.

And somehow, through all of it, you became my favourite person to come back to.

And this is only the beginning...
