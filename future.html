Chapter Three

One day...

When the distance is finally just a story we tell.
♡   ✦   ♡

One day, I hope the distance between us is just a memory.

I want the boring days with you.

The sleepy mornings, the random grocery runs, the arguments about absolutely nothing, sitting next to each other without having to say anything, and all the tiny moments that we currently have to miss.

I want to be able to look for you in the room
instead of looking at my phone to see when you'll call.

I want Cuppy and Momo to stop complaining about the long-distance situation.

I want Iddi and Dada to finally live together and have all the cuddles they have been waiting for.

And honestly, I want a home that feels like us.

A home full of love, laughter, food, chaos, inside jokes, sleepy hugs, and all the people we love.

I don't know exactly what every part of our future will look like.

And maybe that's what makes it exciting.

There will probably be difficult days. There will be things we disagree about, things we have to learn, and things we have to figure out along the way.

But I know who I want beside me while we figure it all out.

You.
Always you. ♡
