Dear Hemin

♡

And finally, happiest birthday, my love. ♡

I am so, so proud of you.

Everything that you do somehow makes me feel proud of you all the time. I want to brag about you to everyone and be like,

"You would never get such an amazing husband ever in your life because you simply aren't that lucky."

And yes, I will continue taking full credit for getting this lucky myself.

I genuinely wish the very best for you. I want you to do everything you dream of doing, and I want to be there through all of it — cheering for you, annoying you, supporting you, and reminding you how ridiculously proud I am of you.

I also want to say I'm sorry.

I know you're busy right now, and I know how important everything you're doing is. And because it's important to you, it's important to me too.

So I'm sorry for the times when I was mean, reluctant, or made things a little more difficult for you when you were already trying to manage everything at once.

I hope I never make you feel like you have to handle everything alone.

Please don't take too much stress. I want to help you with your work. I want to help you with everything. I genuinely wish I could sometimes just take things off your plate and make everything a little easier for you.

And please, please talk to me whenever you're stressed.

Even if I don't always know exactly what to say, I want to listen. And if I ever don't listen to you properly, TELL ME TO SIT AND LISTEN. I mean it.

I want to be someone you can come to, especially on the days when everything feels like too much.

I also don't want to promise you that our future will be happy all the time.

Because one day we're going to start living together, and you're probably going to discover approximately seventeen new flaws in me that you currently don't know about. And honestly, I'm lowkey nervous about it.

I'm curious. I'm excited. I'm worried. I'm wondering what we'll be like when we're around each other every single day, when there is no distance to blame, and when we have to figure out all the boring, difficult, annoying parts of life together.

But I'm sure we'll figure it out.

Because we make a great match. That's true as hell. And honestly, nobody who knows us should even try to argue with that. 🔪

I don't need us to be perfect.

I just want us to keep choosing each other, keep talking to each other, keep learning each other, and keep finding our way back to each other — even on the difficult days.

I want the life we keep talking about.

The home. The family. The chaos. The cuddles. Cuppy and Momo finally getting what they want. All the ordinary days that right now feel so far away.

And most importantly, I want you in all of it.

So finally, happiest birthday to my favourite person.

I love you more than I know how to explain.

And I am so, so lucky that somehow, out of everyone in this world, I got you.

Happy birthday, Hemin. ♡

Always yours,
Iddi
